## Introduction

**CT-Marks** is a user-friendly web-based marks management system designed to simplify **marks entry, automatic calculation, verification, result publishing, and academic performance tracking**. It provides role-based access for administrators, faculty, students, and parents while focusing on **accuracy, transparency, fairness, security, accessibility, and minimal user input**. The system also integrates guided tutorials, personalized preferences, reminders, notifications, and automated features to make academic management easier for both technical and non-technical users.

# Problem Statement of the CT-Marks Web App :-

1. Miscalculation and incorrect data entry can lead to errors and unnecessary time consumption.
2. Accidental submission, modification or data loss can affect the accuracy of results.
3. Complicated interfaces can make the system difficult to operate, especially for less technically experienced users.
4. Complicated formula calculations can lead to human calculation errors.
5. Lack of an integrated platform for different users with different preferences, roles and access requirements.
6. Lack of transparency and biased evaluation can lead to unfair results and injustice to students.
7. Lack of automation and integration of advanced technologies limits the efficiency of the existing educational system.
8. Weak password management and improper access control can lead to security and privacy breaches.
9. Difficulty in tracking changes made to marks can reduce accountability and transparency.
10. Lack of proper correction and complaint mechanisms can make it difficult to resolve genuine mistakes or disputes.
11. Repetitive manual tasks such as calculation, report generation and result preparation consume time.
12. Lack of personalized assistance can make technical terms and system operations difficult for first-time users.

# Users and their roles:-

## 1. Administrator [HODs, Principal, Examination Department, etc.]

Roles:

* Viewing marks and accessing results for transparency and unbiased monitoring.
* Publishing results and report cards.
* Providing notices for result declaration and other academic activities.
* Receiving and responding to complaint/request applications.
* Managing users, roles and permissions.
* Monitoring important activities performed by users.
* Managing correction and re-evaluation requests.
* Monitoring audit logs and system activities.
* Managing departments, semesters, sections and subjects.
* Managing examination schedules and result deadlines.
* Maintaining system security and data backup.
* Integrating the marksheet with APAAR of a student, wherever applicable and supported.

---

## 2. Teachers, Professors and Faculty

Roles:

* Entering, calculating and modifying marks according to permitted stages.
* Submitting marksheets and progress reports.
* Reviewing and verifying marks before submission.
* Maintaining personal notes attached to student records for academic purposes.
* Preparing student performance/behaviour summaries for academic planning, where permitted by institutional policy.
* Receiving notifications and filing complaint/request applications.
* Scheduling examinations with the help of the integrated calendar.
* Reviewing pending marks and submission deadlines.
* Maintaining accurate records and responsible use of audit logs.
* Requesting corrections to already published marks through the proper approval process.

---

## 3. Students and Parents/Guardians

Roles:

* Viewing and downloading marks and report sheets.
* Tracking academic performance and progress.
* Receiving academic notifications and result announcements.
* Viewing examination schedules and important deadlines.
* Filing request, correction or complaint applications.
* Tracking the status of submitted applications.
* Managing permitted personal preferences and profile information.

* Role-based access to prevent unauthorized modification.
* Automatic calculation to reduce manual calculation errors.
* Marks validation before submission.
* Review screen before publishing results.
* Audit logs for important actions.
* Record of previous and updated marks during approved corrections.
* Date, time and user identification for important changes.

* Complaint and request mechanism for students and parents.
* Optional anonymous / blind evaluation where applicable.
* Prevention of unauthorized access to student records.
* Transparent result publishing process.
* No direct modification of finalized marks without proper authorization.
* System-generated calculations instead of manually entered totals.

# Main Design Principle:-

**Enter Less → Calculate Automatically → Verify → Publish Safely → Track Transparently**

The system should prioritize:

**Simplicity + Fairness + Security + Accessibility + Automation + Transparency**
